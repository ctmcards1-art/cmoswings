How a swing review works

  1. Film three to five game-speed swings, one angle from the side and one from behind.
  2. Check out and paste a link to the video, or send it to @CmoSwings on Instagram.
  3. Within 72 hours you get a recorded breakdown with voice-over, the adjustments to make and the drills to fix them.

Your coach

Every lesson starts with your swing as it is today: an assessment, drill work that targets what needs fixing, and video so you can see the change. Can't make it in person? Send a video for an online swing review.
